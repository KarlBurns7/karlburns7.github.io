The Desk

Karl Burns's personal assistant. One user, his own Mac.

The Desk is a private, single-user assistant that Karl Burns built for himself. It isn't offered to anyone else, and there is no sign-up.

It runs on Karl's own Mac mini. With his permission it reads his Gmail to surface what needs his attention: replies he owes, actions, bills, school updates and dates to add to his calendar. It shows these on his phone and in his private notes.

Email is only sent when Karl approves it

  • The assistant can prepare a Gmail draft for a reply Karl asked for.
  • Nothing is sent until Karl reviews the exact message on his own paired phone and taps Approve. There is then a 30-second window to undo it.
  • It never sends email on its own, and it never sends to anyone Karl hasn't seen on the approval screen.

Google access it uses

  • Read Gmail (gmail.readonly): to read messages and find what needs Karl's attention, and to check whether a reply has already gone.
  • Manage drafts and send (gmail.compose): to create a draft of a reply and to send it after Karl approves it.

How this data is handled is in the privacy policy.
