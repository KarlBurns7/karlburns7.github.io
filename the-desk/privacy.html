Privacy policy

The Desk · Effective 25 October 2026

In short: The Desk is Karl Burns's personal assistant, and Karl is its only user. It reads his own Gmail for him, keeps what it learns on his own Mac mini and in his own private notes, and sends an email only after he approves that exact message. Nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or used for anything else.

Who this covers

The Desk is not a public service. It is used by one person, Karl Burns, with his own Google account. No one else can sign in to it.

What Google data it accesses, and why

  • gmail.readonly: read Karl's email. Used to find messages that need his attention (replies he owes, actions, bills, school updates and dates to add to his calendar), to show them to him, and to check whether a reply has already been sent so nothing goes twice.
  • gmail.compose: manage drafts and send. Used to create a draft of a reply Karl asked for, and to send that draft only after Karl reviews it on his own paired phone and taps Approve. There is a 30-second window to undo before it goes.

The Desk does not delete email, change Gmail settings, or send to anyone Karl hasn't seen on the approval screen.

Where the data goes

  • Karl's own Mac mini. The assistant runs there. The Google sign-in token is stored on that machine in a file only Karl's account can read.
  • Karl's own private notes. Short summaries of emails that matter are kept in Karl's personal notes folder, which syncs to his own Google Drive.
  • Anthropic's Claude service, under Karl's own account. To read and summarise messages, the assistant sends the relevant email text to Claude, the AI model Karl uses. This is only to provide these features to Karl.

No one else receives the data. It is not sold, not shared with advertisers or data brokers, and not used for advertising.

Limited Use

The Desk's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How long it is kept

  • Email summaries in Karl's notes are kept until he deletes them.
  • Chat history and approval records (what was sent, when, and that Karl approved it) stay on the Mac mini, which only Karl uses. The retention he has chosen for chat history is 180 days, and he can delete any of it at any time.
  • The Google sign-in token is kept until access is revoked.

How to revoke access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions: choose The Desk, then Remove access. The assistant then can't read or send Gmail until it is signed in again.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it.
