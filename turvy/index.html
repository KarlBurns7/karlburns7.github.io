Turvy

Upside-down space runner

What it is

Turvy is a small astronaut with a big battery, running laps of a ring-shaped space station where the ceiling is just another floor. Crash into something and the battery rewinds you a few seconds, so you can take two. Every rewind costs charge, and coins put it back. When the battery runs flat, that's the run. Short runs, bright colours, no accounts, and nothing leaves your phone. No ads and no in-app purchases. The whole game is in the download.

How to play

  • Swipe left or rightChange lane.
  • TapJump.
  • Swipe downRoll under things.
  • Swipe upFlip, and run on the ceiling when the floor gets busy.
  • CoinsEvery coin charges the rewind battery.
  • CrashWhile there is charge, a crash rewinds you a few seconds and you go again. When the battery is flat, that's the run.

Questions

Do I need an account?
No. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to sign in to.
Are there ads or in-app purchases?
No. The whole game is in the download.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Turvy never connects to the internet.
How do I reset my best distance?
Delete the app and install it again. Your best distance and settings are stored on your phone only, and they go when the app does.
Does Turvy support Reduce Motion?
Yes. Turn on Reduce Motion in iOS Settings, or use the switch in Turvy's own settings. No shake, fewer sparks. The flip still rolls.
